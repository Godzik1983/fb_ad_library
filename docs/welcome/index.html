How To Pin

1
Click the puzzle icon in the top-right corner of Chrome.
2
Find FB AD LIBRARY in the extensions list.
3
Click the pin icon, then keep it visible in your browser toolbar.

Why Pin It

Pinned = faster workflow.
Launch insights instantly, save useful ads to your library, and review patterns without searching for the extension each time.

Quick Start

1
Open Meta Ad Library and browse ads in your niche.
2
Click an ad card, then open FB AD LIBRARY from the toolbar.
3
Save strong creatives and review your personal ad library later.
